      <![CDATA[<p><span >Holi isn't just a festival of colours, it's a celebration of stories, heritage, and flavours passed down through generations. At its heart is </span><em   >thandai</em><span >, the iconic North Indian cooler made with saffron, nuts, and spices, designed to refresh the body and spirit under the spring sun.</span></p>
<p class=""  >This year, we’re reimagining that tradition with a unique twist rooted in cultural richness: <strong  >Paan Chocolate Thandai</strong>, inspired by the timeless charm of <em  >Banarasi Paan</em>.</p>
<p class=""  >The Banarasi paan isn’t just a mouth freshener, it’s a cultural symbol. Originating from the ghats of Varanasi, this betel leaf preparation is known for its elaborate ingredients: rose petals, gulkand, fennel, coconut, and areca nut, all wrapped in a fresh paan leaf. It's a ritual of hospitality, a post-meal delicacy, and a nostalgic nod to Indian street culture.</p>
<p class=""  >We bring that heritage into a chocolate bar and now, into a Holi drink.</p>
<p>To make this fusion thandai:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<span>Take some fresh paan (<meta charset="utf-8">
<em>Red betel leaf)</em></span><span> leaves and chop them finely.</span>
</li>
</ul>
<p><span><img loading="lazy" src="/images/journal/Paan_Chocolate_Thandai2-960w.webp" alt="" ></span></p>
<ul>
<li><span>Add the chopped leaves into warm vegan milk of your choice to release all those earthy flavours.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span><img loading="lazy" src="/images/journal/Paan_Chocolate_Thandai3-960w.webp" alt="" ></span></p>
<ul>
<li><span>Break 1 (or 2) bars of Darkins Banarasi Paan Bar into small pieces and mix it into the milk (or vegan alternatives) </span></li>
<li><span>Stir well and don’t forget to strain the mixture for a smooth texture.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span><img loading="lazy" src="/images/journal/Paan_Chocolate_Thandai4-960w.webp" alt="" ></span></p>
<ul>
<li><span>For a refreshing twist, add crushed ice and pulse it, or if you prefer layers, add the ice directly to your glass!</span></li>
<li><span>Enjoy your chocolatey delight made with 100% natural, vegan Darkins Chocolate!</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span><img loading="lazy" src="/images/journal/Paan_Chocolate_Thandai5-960w.webp" alt="" ></span></p>
<p class=""  >This is Holi in a glass, with a sweet Banarasi soul.</p>]]>
